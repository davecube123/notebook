# Almanac

A private, offline-first notebook PWA. No build step, no server, no account — everything lives in your browser's IndexedDB.

## What's inside

- **Notes**: rich editor with checklists, bullets, numbered lists (nest any of them under a checkbox or item with Tab or the → toolbar button; ticking a task ticks its sub-tasks), quotes, headings, code, dividers, links, highlight, bold/italic/underline/strike. Markdown shortcuts work too (`[ ]`, `-`, `1.`, `>`, `#`, ```` ``` ````, `---`, each followed by a space). Tags (nested like `#work/meetings`): the filter row shows your picked and 5 most recent tags, and the Tags button opens every tag to search, sort (A–Z, most used, recent) and pick several. Pinning, archive, trash. Search by text, `#tag`, `from:2026-01-01`, `to:`, `on:`, `is:pinned`, `is:todo`, or the date-range filter. Group by date or tag. Export to Markdown.
- **Daily note & templates**: one note per day (from Today, the ☀︎ button in Notes, or the `D` key), with links to the day before and after. Research-backed templates, each with a "Why it works" section and sources: Daily note (if–then plan, a feeling, three good things, a free write, tomorrow's list), Weekly review, Decision journal (with a pre-mortem), CBT thought record, Best possible self, Learning note, Conversation debrief. Make your own templates: write one from scratch (Templates → New template), start from a built-in ("Make my own version"), or save any note as one from its ⋯ menu. Your templates are edited in the normal editor, stay out of the notes list and search, and are included in backups. Choose which template the daily note uses. An untouched note made from a template is discarded.
- **One Thing**: one must-finish task per day. Checking it records the time. You get a streak, completion rate, a 6‑month heatmap and a full log. If yesterday's wasn't finished, you can carry it over.
- **Habits**: each habit has a rhythm: daily, or a number of times a week, month or quarter. Today's list shows what is still due, with the day's One Thing always first under Every day (it can only be ticked once written) and counting toward the win; grouped by rhythm, with how many are left and by when; a habit that has met its target moves to "Done for now" until its next period. Checking every daily habit (including the One Thing) plus enough checks in all (5 by default, changeable) wins the day: a ring fills, a celebration marks the win, and won days make the streak, with Showed up / Win / Perfect levels. The Progress tab shows days won this week, each weekly, monthly and quarterly habit against the time left, every habit's chain in its own rhythm, and a heatmap of won days. Tap a habit for its page.
- **Calendar**: a month of moments. Write a line about what happened on a day and it gets an icon (picked from your words, or chosen from 16 named icons and 36 more under "More icons"). Each day shows its icons; tap a day to read, add or edit its moments, plus birthdays, people you saw and notes written that day. Swipe to change month. Logs from the old trackers were copied in as moments.
- **People**: three tabs per person: Personality, Log (encounters) and Notes. Birthday countdowns and "reconnect" reminders still use details saved in earlier versions.
- **Personality reading**: a one-screen dashboard (Big Five meters you tap to rate, tiles for temperament, Enneagram and zodiac, and the top do's and don'ts), with every detail one tap away in a sheet. Four lenses, each explained as you fill it in and each with a do / don't guide on how to relate to the person. Big Five rated 1–5 with the 30 NEO PI-R facets (Costa & McCrae); Keirsey temperament from *Please Understand Me II* (4 guided questions); Enneagram core desire, fear and passion, with a 2-question test (Riso & Hudson's QUEST method) and a full type profile (centre, wing, motivations, levels, stress and growth, virtue, fixation, typical defence); zodiac sign from their birthday or picked, with a disclaimer.
- **People in notes**: type `@` in a note to mention someone; the link stores that exact person, so two people with the same name never mix. Each person has a Notes tab with the notes about them, plus notes that contain their name but aren't linked yet (Link / Not them). `@New name` adds someone to People on the spot.
- **Feelings** (off by default; turn it on in Settings → Features, and past check-ins are kept): check in through the day using the Mood Meter (pleasant/unpleasant × high/low energy), then pick up to 3 precise words from a 137-word vocabulary with definitions, to practise affect labelling and emotional granularity. Optional intensity, energy, drive and desire levels, a "because…" note and what you need. After each check-in you get one small next step for that zone. The overview shows a mood map, five weeks of mood dots, 30-day trends, your vocabulary with new words to try, and what tends to go with your better days.
- **Today**: dashboard with the one thing, habits, today's moments, people nudges and **Today's note**: today's daily note from the template you pick (its sections, tap one to write there; your own templates' prompts are found whether they're headings, bold lines, lines ending in ":" or "?", or "Label: " lines) with a quick capture that adds time-stamped lines to it.
- Global search (`/` or `⌘K`), keyboard shortcuts, light/dark theme, accent colors, passcode lock (a privacy screen; data isn't encrypted), JSON backup and restore, installable and works offline.

## Run locally

```sh
npx http-server -c-1 .   # or any static server
```

Open http://localhost:8080. To deploy, host the folder on any static host (GitHub Pages, Netlify…). The service worker needs HTTPS (or localhost). Bump `VERSION` in `sw.js` when you ship changes.
